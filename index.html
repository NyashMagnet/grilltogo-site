Franchise Application & Inquiries

To apply, please copy the questions below and email your detailed answers to jamali@robogrill.co. We look forward to learning more about you.

1. Name & Contact

– Your full name, phone number, and email address.

2. Territory & Why

– What city or region do you want, and why is it the perfect spot for a Grill to Go?

3. Your Track Record

– In 2–3 sentences, sum up your most relevant food-service or retail management wins.

4. Financials

– State your total net worth AND liquid capital available. (Keep it simple: “£X net worth, £Y ready to invest.”)

5. The X-Factor

– What makes you the perfect Grill to Go operator? (Give us your 3-sentence pitch.)

6. Timeline & Commitment

– When could you realistically open your doors? Any other commitments we should know about?

7. Support Needs

– What’s the one area you’ll want our help most? (Training, marketing, tech setup, ops, etc.)

8. Referral Source

– How did you hear about us? (LinkedIn, friend, billboard, carrier pigeon…)

Our franchise team carefully reviews every application. We appreciate you taking the time to apply and will be in touch regarding the next steps within 7-10 business days.

Please send your completed application to:
